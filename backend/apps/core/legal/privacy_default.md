## Who we are

{company}. We organise tours in Kyrgyzstan and are responsible for the data described here.

## What we collect

- **Request form:** your name, email and/or phone (WhatsApp), the tour, rough dates, number of travellers and your message.
- **Guest reviews:** your name, country, rating and text (published on the site after we check it), and optionally your email — never shown, only used if we have a question about your review.
- **Technical data with a request or review:** IP address and browser type, to protect the forms from spam. They are deleted after 30 days.
- **Campaign tags** (for example utm_source) and the page you sent a request from — to know which pages and ads work.
- **Our own visit counters:** how many times each tour page was opened and how many times WhatsApp was tapped, per day. These counters contain no personal data.
- **Analytics (only with your consent where required):** pages visited, approximate location (country/city), device type and how you found the site, via Google Analytics 4.

We do not take payments on this website and never ask for card details here.

## Why we use it

- To answer your request and prepare your trip (steps before a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- To publish your review, which you send to us for that purpose (your consent, Art. 6(1)(a)).
- To protect the forms from spam and abuse (our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).
- To understand how the site is used and improve it — only after you accept analytics cookies if you are in the EU/EEA, the UK or Switzerland (consent, Art. 6(1)(a)).

## Who else processes data

We never sell your data. We use these services to run the site:

- Hosting: Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany — stores the website, requests and reviews.
- Email delivery: Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) — sends us the notification about your request and you a confirmation.
- Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd.) — visit statistics, only if you allow it.
- Map tiles: OpenFreeMap, OpenTopoMap and Amazon Web Services (elevation data) — your browser loads map images from them, so they see your IP address. No cookies.
- WhatsApp (Meta) — only if you message us there; WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.

Some of these providers are outside your country; they use standard safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Cookies

- **Analytics cookies** (_ga, _ga_*): set by Google Analytics, up to 2 years, only after you click “Accept”. You can change your choice any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.
- **Necessary storage:** a security cookie (csrftoken) protects the forms; your cookie choice and campaign tags are kept in your browser (localStorage / sessionStorage). These do not track you and need no consent.

## How long we keep it

- IP address and browser type: 30 days.
- Requests: up to 3 years, to answer follow-up questions and keep records of trips, then deleted. Spam is deleted after 30 days.
- Reviews: while they are on the site, or until you ask us to remove them.
- Analytics data: 14 months.
