## What we collect

- **Request form:** your name, email, phone or messenger contact, the tour, dates, number of people and your message.

We do not ask for payment details on this website.

## Why we use it

- To answer your request and prepare your trip (steps before a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- To protect the form from spam and abuse (our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).
- To understand how the site is used and improve it — only after you accept analytics cookies if you are in the EU/EEA, the UK or Switzerland (consent, Art. 6(1)(a)).
